Add to Chrome · it's free

Also works in Edge, Brave, Arc and Opera on computers. No account needed.

Using Edge? Click "Allow extensions from other stores" when asked.

Set up in 30 seconds

  1. Add to Chrome
    1

    Click "Add to Chrome", then "Add extension".

  2. 2

    Open any YouTube channel. A Creators + button appears on the left.

  3. 3

    Click it to see the channel's stats, graphs and top videos.

Tip: click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome and pin Creators +. Its icon shows what's trending on any website.
