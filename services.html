Questions

Where does the data come from?
From Like Polling's own daily records of open data: Wikipedia page views (Wikimedia Foundation) and worldwide news coverage (The GDELT Project). The ideas are written by AI (Google Gemini) and can contain mistakes.
Does the report include YouTube or news headlines?
No. YouTube rankings and news headlines belong to YouTube and the publishers, so they stay free on likepolling.com and are never sold. You pay for our work: collecting, organising and explaining open data.
What do you keep about me?
Nothing. Lemon Squeezy handles the payment and your email. Like Polling has no accounts and stores no buyer data. See the privacy policy.
Can I get a refund?
Yes, within 14 days if the report isn't useful to you. See the refund policy.
