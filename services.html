    Questions

    Where does the data come from?

    Open data only. Country reports: Like Polling's daily records of Wikipedia views (Wikimedia Foundation) and worldwide news coverage (The GDELT Project), with ideas written by AI (Google Gemini). Artist and company reports: Wikipedia views in 17 languages, compared with similar pages.

    Does the report include YouTube or news headlines?

    No. YouTube rankings and news headlines belong to YouTube and the publishers, so they stay free on likepolling.com and are never sold. You pay for our work: collecting, organising and explaining open data.

    What do you keep about me?

    Nothing. Our checkout partner handles the payment and your email. Like Polling has no accounts and stores no buyer data. Privacy policy
