Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

Like Polling keeps no personal data about you. There are no accounts and no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share data.

  • We count page visits and clicks per country, without cookies and without saving your IP address.
  • Your language, country and report key stay in your browser, not on our servers.
  • Payments go through Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy. They receive your name, email and card details; we don't.
  • Channel searches only look up public YouTube statistics.
  • Ad cards hold only public business details (brand name, website, tagline), saved so we can show the ad.
  • The Creators + browser extension sends only the YouTube channel you open, to show its statistics. It doesn't read anything else and doesn't track you.

Their privacy policies: Gumroad · Lemon Squeezy
