Privacy policy

For likepolling.com and the Creators + browser extension. Last updated November 2026.

Creators + extension

Creators + shows Like Polling's YouTube rankings and channel statistics on YouTube and in its toolbar popup.

  • What it sends: when you open a YouTube channel, video or Short, the extension reads that channel's public ID or @handle from the page and sends only that to likepolling.com to fetch its statistics. Rankings requests send only the country code you picked.
  • What it never sends: page contents, comments, messages, full URLs, search terms or your browsing history. It does not run on any website other than www.youtube.com.
  • What it stores on your device: your chosen country, whether the floating button is shown, where you dragged it, and a short-lived cache of statistics (cleared when the browser closes). Nothing is stored in an account; there are no accounts.
  • No tracking: no analytics, advertising IDs or cookies are used by the extension. We don't sell or share data.
  • Permissions: "storage" for the settings above, and access to www.youtube.com (to recognise the channel and show the panel) and www.likepolling.com (to fetch statistics).

likepolling.com

  • We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify individual visitors.
  • Searches you make for YouTube channels are sent to our server to look up public YouTube statistics, and the channel's public statistics are saved in our database.
  • Your language choice is kept in your browser's local storage.

Buying a report

  • Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad, our Merchants of Record. Your name, email, payment and billing details go to them, under their privacy policies (Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad), not to Like Polling.
  • There are no accounts. To open a report, your browser sends your license key to our server, which checks it with Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad and doesn't store it. Your browser keeps the key in local storage so the report opens next time.
  • If you arrived through a marketing link (for example ?ref=linkedin), the services page keeps that word in your browser's local storage and adds it to the checkout, so we can see which channel led to a sale. It identifies the channel, not you.
  • Requests for custom work or sponsorship are ordinary emails to lucaspaiva@likepolling.com.

Data sources

Statistics come from the public YouTube Data API and Google News. Estimated earnings are a range based on views, the channel's country, each video's topic and how many views come from Shorts (typical creator-reported rates per 1,000 views), not real revenue.
