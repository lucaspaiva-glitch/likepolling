What these numbers mean

An estimate of how many people are watching YouTube at the same moment in each country, on any screen: phones, computers, tablets and TVs. It counts people, not devices or monthly users.

Modelled, not measured: YouTube does not publish live viewer counts. Each figure is YouTube users in that country (Google's ad-reach figures, late 2025, via DataReportal) × about 80 minutes of viewing per person per day, spread over the day by a typical hourly viewing pattern.

Recalculated every 15 minutes for each country's local time. Large countries (United States, Canada, Brazil, Mexico, Australia, Indonesia) are split across their time zones by population. Read the figures as an order of magnitude, not an exact count.
